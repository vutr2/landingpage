The Problem

The Silent Killer
has a diagnostic gap

Pancreatic cancer kills silently. Existing tools treat each scan as an isolated snapshot — missing the progression signals that could save lives.

⏱

Delayed Detection

80% of patients are diagnosed at late, metastatic stages when surgical options are no longer available and survival odds collapse.

📊

Static AI Models

Current clinical AI treats each data point in isolation. It cannot model temporal evolution — missing the disease trajectory that predicts early-stage risk.

🔬

Untapped Biomarker Data

Longitudinal biomarker dynamics — CA19-9, LYVE1, REG1B — contain rich progression signals. Today these are rarely modeled at scale or in combination.

🏥

No Clinical Decision Support

Oncologists lack explainable, physics-grounded risk scores that account for individual tumor growth kinetics and inflammatory response over time.

13%
5-Year Survival Rate

Despite decades of research, pancreatic cancer survivability remains among the lowest of all cancers. Detected at Stage I, survival rates exceed 80%. The detection gap is the central challenge — and it is solvable.

The Science

Coupled biomarker dynamics
constrained by biology

Our model encodes known laws of tumor biology directly into the neural network's loss function — making predictions that are physically consistent, not just statistically correlated.

Architecture

GPU-Accelerated PINN

Native CUDA integration for high-throughput longitudinal ODE/PDE simulation. Runs real-time inference via NVIDIA TensorRT on clinical hardware.

Neural networks constrained by actual tumor growth mathematics — the Fisher-KPP equation governs spatial diffusion; logistic dynamics govern growth kinetics.
