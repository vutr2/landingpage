Mission

Help the healthcare field study and follow blood cancer earlier — not sell another software contract.

Blood cancers often present late. Years earlier, many people already carry expanding clones in CHIP. Scoring those clones only by gene name and a single VAF snapshot is too coarse for research and too late for patients.

C&T Technology builds a continuous, biology-level signal: how fast a clone is growing, and how the specific protein change looks to a foundation model. The intended impact is practical — better research cohorts, clearer lab ranking, and a path toward earlier follow-up for patients.

FOR PATIENTS

Time is the treatment window

If an expanding clone is visible years before a myeloid neoplasm is named, research and follow-up can start while the disease is still a trajectory, not a crisis.

FOR HEALTHCARE

A product labs can run

Input: sequenced variants over time. Output: fitness and mutation-effect scores. That is a product workflow owned by C&T Technology, not a one-off consulting project.

The product

What C&T Technology is building

01

Read the clone

Longitudinal VAF from CHIP / hematology sequencing. Fitness s = d(log VAF)/dt, calibrated against synonymous noise.

02

Read the mutation

Each missense change is mapped to protein coordinates and scored as an ESM-2 residue-level delta, not a gene one-hot.

03

Rank risk

Combine growth rate and protein-level effect so researchers can order clones by aggressiveness, not only by gene fame.

04

Serve healthcare

The destination is a scoring service for labs and research groups — GPU-backed, reproducible, owned as a product by C&T Technology.
