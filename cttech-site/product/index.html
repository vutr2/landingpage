Problem

CHIP is common. Progression is rare. Binary labels waste most of the data.

Clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential (CHIP) is frequent in older adults. Only a small fraction progress to myeloid neoplasm each year. A “will progress” classifier therefore throws away almost every carrier. Clonal fitness — the growth rate of a mutant clone — is measurable in every carrier, sick or not.

SIGNAL

Fitness, not just gene name

s = d(log VAF) / dt from longitudinal variant-allele frequency. Synonymous mutations set the noise floor so we do not rank sequencing error.

REPRESENTATION

Residue-level ESM-2 delta

We do not mean-pool the whole protein. We take ESM-2(mutant)[pos] − ESM-2(wild-type)[pos] at the mutated residue — the local change the model actually sees.

Pipeline

What is running today

01

Cohorts

Lothian Birth Cohorts, SardiNIA, and BioVU missense trajectories with VAF over time.

02

Annotation

Genomic variants mapped to protein substitutions (VEP + Amino_acids / Protein_position when HGVSp is empty).

03

Embed

ESM-2 650M residue embeddings, windowed for long proteins (TET2, ASXL1).

04

Score

Ridge / PCA regression from delta vectors to standardized fitness s_z. BeatAML used as a secondary-AML proxy task.
